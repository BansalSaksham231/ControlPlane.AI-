========================================================================
  mode                     catch-F1   human-escalation   clean-escalation
  performance_only         0.78         43%                 0%
  responsibility_only      0.80         44%                 0%
  cost_only                0.18          7%                 0%
  fused_only               0.82         46%                 0%
  fused_plus_consequence   0.28         11%                 0%
  risk_only_no_confidence  0.88         39%                 0%
  full_pipeline            0.88         39%                 0%

  Best flat single-score mode ('fused_only') catches risky cases at F1=0.82 (recall 0.69) — it misses much because it sees only one dimension. The full pipeline reaches F1=0.88 (recall 0.98) while escalating 0% of clean traffic to a human, and assigns a proportionate tier rather than a binary flag. Confidence-awareness makes no difference on THIS synthetic set (58 BLOCK/HUMAN_REVIEW either way): the lexical NLI produces confident contradictions and low-risk unverifieds, so the 'high-risk / low-confidence' regime the LOW_CONFIDENCE_HIGH_RISK rule targets is rare here (it is unit-tested directly).
